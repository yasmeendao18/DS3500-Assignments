Glasgow Barren County Kentucky

July the 20, 1862

Mr. Jasper N. Butram

Dear friend,
I seat my self this plesant Sunday eaving to answer your kind request.  I am well at this time hoping when thoes few lines comes to your hand they may find you injoying good health, plesure and happiness.  I have nothing to tell you that would interes you much.
We herd that there is twelve thousand releb crossing the river at Solina but it is rumer but it may be so.  If it is we will find it out in a day or two but it is not Morgan for the last acount we had of him he was up in about Bursville.
From all accounts he will have to smell powder before he gets to steal many more horses when he come threw here he got ever horse that he could get holt of that was any a count but when he runes in to a fight he all ways get a whiping in the scrinish at Tompinsville because his lads was the gretest he was mad he shot a woman because here husban was in the union army and killed too or three childes accidently in the citty.
Standing in the dors our brave and generous Woldford we under stand is coming back in to Brren once more. We never feal proticted unless he is about.  I tell you he has got some of the prttyes boys in redgiment you ever cast your eyes upon.
You say this is the third letter you have wrote me it is the only one that I have ever received from you.  Miluina Denham sends you here best love and compliments.  I dremp the other night that you and Mary Dodson was to be married.
I thought I herd you ask her to have you and she sed she would have you if you would be for southern rits and you told her certainly you could be for south rits.  you must not comply with no such a contract down there in Dicsy.
I must close my letter.  Give my best love to all inquiring friend so nothing more at present.

Yours truly,
Sarah E. Denham

J.N. Butram
