Head Quarters 3rd Division C.C.
July 6th, 1864

Brig. Genl. S.A. Grant
Comdg. Vermont Brigade
6th Army Corps A. of P.

General,
In reply to your letter of the 4th inst. just received I have the honor to state, that Captain James A Sayles 8th N.Y. Cav. was on duty with his regiment during the recent raid, there being too few officers in the 8th N.Y.  Cav.
to allow Genl. Wilson to follow his intention to take him on the Staff: in a severe engagement on the railroad between Blacks & White and Nottaway C.C. where Sayles behaved very gallantly, he must have been wounded or taken prisoner in a temporary advance of the part of the line where his men were posted, who had to fall back again to their original position not being properly sustained.
He was last seen lying behind a log firing his revolver. We all deplore his fate, but sincerely hope and wish that he may not be wounded and if so, not seriously. His name was honorably mentioned in Regimental Brigade and Division reports.
I have the honor to remain, General
Very respectfully
Your Obd. Servt.
(Signed) L. Silbert
Capt. & A.A. Genl.
